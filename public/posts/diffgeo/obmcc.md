abstracts: This note provides a self-contained derivation of the Riemann curvature tensor and the Ricci tensor in an orthonormal basis, using the method of Ricci rotation coefficients. The basic framework of the orthonormal tetrad formalism is first reviewed, followed by detailed proofs of the equivalence between metric compatibility and the antisymmetry of the connection one-forms, and between torsion-freeness and the commutation relations of the basis vectors. Explicit component formulas for the Riemann tensor in terms of the Ricci rotation coefficients are derived, and their translation into differential forms notation is also demonstrated.
